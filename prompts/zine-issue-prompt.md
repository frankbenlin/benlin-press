# Zine Issue Prompt

Create a short editorial issue for Benlin Press focused on public knowledge, accessible publishing, and the future of independent media.

Goal:
- Produce an 8-page zine issue in a concise, readable format.
- Use a thoughtful tone with a clear editorial voice.
- Keep the narrative accessible to a broad audience.

Structure:
1. Title and issue framing
2. Intro to the public press
3. Why independent publishing matters
4. Tools and workflows for everyday publishing
5. Design and print considerations
6. Community and a broader public conversation
7. Closing reflection

Output style:
- Clear headings
- Compact paragraphs
- Strong editorial rhythm
- Premium but approachable tone

Required theme:
Freedom of the press exists when everyone becomes the press.
