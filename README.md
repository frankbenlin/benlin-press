# Benlin Press

Benlin Press is a publishing system for everyday writers, teams, and solopreneurs who want to turn ideas into standardized, print-ready documents.

Our mission is simple: freedom of the press exists when everyone becomes the press.

## What we build

We design writing assistance prompts and reusable skills that help people produce polished, structured documents with less friction:

- personal statements and essays
- business proposals and plans
- meeting summaries and briefs
- research notes and publishing templates
- standard print-ready output formats

## Core principles

- Accessible publishing: clearer writing, easier production
- Standardization: repeatable document quality across contexts
- Printability: outputs that work on paper, in PDF, and in everyday workflows
- Shared authorship: everyone can publish without gatekeepers

## Project structure

- `index.html` — product landing page and brand story
- `styles.css` — editorial, modern design system
- `app.js` — small UI enhancements
- `README.md` — project overview and positioning
- `.gitignore` — standard project ignores

## Local preview

```bash
cd benlin-press
python3 -m http.server 3000
```

Then open `http://localhost:3000`.

## Product direction

Benlin Press sits at the intersection of writing support, publishing tools, and knowledge work. We help turn raw thoughts into complete documents with a consistent format and readable, professional polish.
